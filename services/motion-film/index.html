Motion & Film : Motion design, brand films and reels

Disside is a Hyderabad motion and film studio that puts a brand on screen: logo animations, brand and campaign films, product videos and social reels. Most people now meet brands on a moving screen. We design how the identity should move, then make the films and templates your team can reuse.

What does motion and film include at Disside?

The work includes logo animations and openers, brand and campaign films, social reels and story templates, cinema and digital-screen ads, product and explainer videos, and a simple motion guide. A static logo dropped into a video rarely looks right. We design how the mark appears, how type and graphics move, and which pace and music fit the tone.

Our motion work includes the Keep Pushing film for the Supervek x Jägermeister collaboration, the identity of D2R Films — the production house behind Go Goa Gone — whose logo needed to work as an animated opener, and brand work for Over the Wicket Films. For Uber in Hyderabad, key visuals were also adapted into cinema ads.

How is a film or a reel made?

A logo animation or a set of reel templates takes one to two weeks. A short brand or campaign film with a shoot usually takes three to six weeks from script to final edit. We script, storyboard and design the graphics, and we work with trusted directors, editors and animators for shoots and post-production. You receive files ready for Instagram, YouTube, cinema and digital screens.

This work is strongest when it follows a visual identity and sits inside a campaign. We work with Hyderabad brands and with teams in Mumbai, Bengaluru and other Indian cities, including media and film studios and D2C labels. Reviews can happen on a call; shoots are planned where the story needs them.

Who is motion and film for?

Brands launching a campaign, film and media studios that need an identity which moves, and anyone growing on Instagram and YouTube who wants reels that look like their brand rather than a template from elsewhere. Disside has been a Hyderabad design studio since 2012; motion is how that design practice meets a screen that does not stand still.

If you already have a date for a reel or a film, the calculator on this page will give you a first sense of scope, or write to hello@disside.com. If you already have footage, we can design titles, graphics and an edit around it; if you do not, we can plan a shoot.

Frequently asked questions

What does motion design include at Disside?

Motion design includes logo animations, animated type and graphics, social reels and story templates, cinema and digital-screen ads, and short brand or product films, all in your brand style. A simple motion guide can come with the files, so your team can reuse the same moves later. The brief decides which of these we make first.

Do you shoot films or only animate?

Both. We design and animate in-house, and for live-action shoots we work with trusted directors, camera crews and editors while we lead the creative direction. You still have one point of contact. Finished work is delivered in the formats each channel needs, from a square reel to a cinema master, without you having to recut it yourselves.

How long does a brand film or reel take?

Logo animations and reel-template sets are typically a one-to-two-week job. When a short brand or campaign film includes a shoot, script to final edit is usually three to six weeks. Approvals and the availability of a location or a cast can stretch that, and we plan for them.

What should a Hyderabad brand budget for motion and film?

Fees depend on whether we are animating, shooting, or both, and on the length and number of films. A logo animation and a set of reel templates sit at one end of the range; a campaign film with a shoot sits at the other. We scope this in a proposal rather than offering a single list price.

Can you make films and reels for a brand based in Mumbai?

Yes. We make motion work from Hyderabad for brands in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR and other Indian cities, and we can shoot on location when the story needs it. Reviews of cuts and animations happen on a call. Files are delivered ready for the channels you use, so your local team can publish without a further edit.

Have you made motion work for campaigns and studios?

Yes. We made the Keep Pushing film for the Supervek x Jägermeister collaboration, designed an identity for D2R Films that had to work as an animated opener, and made brand work for Over the Wicket Films. For Uber in Hyderabad, campaign visuals were adapted into cinema ads. Each of these followed the same visual system as the still work.

Will we get templates our social team can reuse?

Yes, when you want that included. We design reel and story templates in your brand style, and a short motion guide that explains how the logo, type and music should behave. Your team can then make new episodes without starting from a blank file.

Do we need a finished identity before commissioning a film?

It helps. A film has to use a logo, type and colour, and those should already be decided. If they are not, we can design a focused identity first, or as a short first stage of the same project. Commissioning a film against a weak or missing identity usually means the work has to be redone when the brand is.

What do you need from us to start a motion project?

A brief for what the film must say and where it will run, your current brand files, and any footage or product you already have. For a shoot, we will also need a sense of locations, people and timing. We then come back with a script or a storyboard and a clear production plan.
