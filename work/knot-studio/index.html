About the Knot Studio project

Knot Studio is a wedding photography studio focused on capturing the moments and stories that make each celebration unique.

Disside developed its visual identity and digital presence, creating a cohesive brand language that reflects the studio’s creative approach and storytelling sensibility.

Key deliverables

  • Brand Identity: Logo and wordmark, colour palette, typography and graphic language.
  • Stationery Design: Branded stationery and communication materials.
  • Website: Website design and development to showcase the studio’s work and photography portfolio.

About

Disside is a branding and design studio in Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad. Since 2012 we have helped Indian businesses, from new startups to listed companies, build brands people remember: strategy, identity, packaging, websites, campaigns, spaces and film.

Brand Strategy

Good design starts with a clear idea. Our brand strategy work helps you decide who you are for, what makes you different and how you should sound. We turn this into a short brand story, naming ideas and messages your team can use every day.

Brand Identity & Visual System

We design logos and brand identities that work everywhere: logo, colours, fonts, icons, photo style and brand guidelines. Already have a brand that feels old? Our rebranding service refreshes it without losing what people already trust.

Packaging Design

Our packaging design helps products stand out on store shelves and on quick-commerce apps. We design labels, boxes, pouches and full product ranges, and we prepare print-ready files with the right legal details.

Website Design

We build fast, mobile-first websites and interactive scrollytelling sites (see ideas) that are easy to update and easy to find on Google. Every site comes with clear pages, enquiry and WhatsApp buttons, and basic SEO set up from day one. We also run digital marketing and SEO.

Campaigns, Signage and Film

From city hoardings to social media, our marketing campaigns help launches get noticed. We design signage, stores and office spaces, and make motion graphics, brand films and product videos.

Industries we work in

We have designed for FMCG and food, restaurants, cafes and beverages, tech and SaaS startups, real estate, D2C, fashion and lifestyle, mobility and EV, hospitality and wellness, media and film, education, healthcare, industrial and manufacturing, architecture and interiors, finance and consulting and textiles and apparel.
